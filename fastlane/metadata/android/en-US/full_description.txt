Super Artemis streams games, apps or your whole desktop from a PC running <a href="https://github.com/ClassicOldSong/Apollo">Apollo</a> (or Sunshine) to your Android phone or tablet. It builds on <a href="https://github.com/ClassicOldSong/moonlight-android">Artemis</a> and Moonlight and adds the features people kept asking for, in one app.

<b>Why Super Artemis</b>
* Microphone streaming to Apollo, off by default so no battery or privacy indicator is used until you unmute it from the menu
* A Quick Menu you arrange yourself: pick and reorder actions, choose list, grid or compact, with Disconnect and Quit kept in their own red section
* A quick-action bar with your favourite actions and keys as small buttons in any screen corner
* Custom keys recorded on the built-in keyboard, with names and icons, no JSON files
* Configurable 3, 4 and 5-finger taps and double taps
* Leave a stream your way: disconnect, resume when you come back, or keep it in picture-in-picture
* Backup and restore of every setting, with a preview that keeps the PCs already on your device
* Settings search and an optional Material You style on Android 12 and later

<b>Also included</b>
* Up to 4K 120 FPS HDR with H.264, HEVC and AV1
* Keyboard, mouse, touch, stylus (S Pen) and controller input
* Network drop grace period so short Wi-Fi drops do not end the session
* Open source (GPLv3), no ads and no tracking

<b>Quick setup</b>
# Install Apollo on your PC from https://github.com/ClassicOldSong/Apollo/releases
# Open the Apollo Web UI on your PC and finish its first-time setup
# Tap your PC in Super Artemis and enter the PIN in the Apollo Web UI
# Start streaming

Super Artemis is an independent fork and is not affiliated with the Artemis or Moonlight projects. Credits for everyone whose work it builds on are in the app under About and in the README.
